Privacy Policy for 2friend

Last Updated: November 2026

2friend ("we", "our", or "us") provides the 2friend social media short-video and messaging application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use 2friend.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Information you provide when signing up or creating a profile, such as your name, email address, and profile details.
  • Content You Share: Short videos (up to 1 minute in 720p or 1080p), 24-hour stories, captions, hashtags, likes, and one-on-one text messages that you post or send in the app.
  • Notification & Technical Data: Push notification tokens (used to deliver app notifications) and basic technical or diagnostic data needed for video playback and app stability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and run user accounts and allow users to search for profiles and view shared videos and stories.
  • To support one-on-one text messaging and deliver push notifications.
  • To maintain app performance, security, and playback reliability.
  • To display advertisements through advertising partners.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

2friend may use third-party services for infrastructure, authentication, push notifications, and advertisements (including AppLovin MAX, Google, Unity, and Meta). These services may process device identifiers or technical usage data under their own privacy policies.

4. App Features & Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect user data transmitted and stored across our services. 2friend focuses on short videos, 24-hour stories, copyright-free music, and one-on-one text chat.

5. Children's Privacy

2friend is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

6. Contact & Data Deletion Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request account/data deletion, please contact us at: support@truefriend.app
